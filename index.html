Denial Pattern Audit

A fixed-scope starting point — not a retainer.

Send your top 15–20 denied procedure codes from the last 6–12 months. Hinoshi Group analyzes the pattern — which codes, which payers, which reasons — and returns a prioritized report showing where your denial-driven revenue loss is concentrated and why.

  • Deliverable: A written report ranking denial losses by procedure and payer, with root-cause analysis and recommended fixes
  • Timeline: 5–7 business days from data receipt
  • Investment: $1,500 flat fee
  • What we need from you: A denial/remittance export — no system access, no ongoing engagement required

This is the fastest way to see what Hinoshi Group finds. If the audit surfaces a bigger opportunity, we'll scope next steps — no obligation beyond the report itself.

Robertson's analytical background includes experience within large health system environments where reimbursement dynamics directly shaped executive financial decisions and strategic planning.
